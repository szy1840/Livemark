# Privacy Policy

Livemark works on files on your Mac.

- It has no account, no telemetry and no update check. It does not send your documents, their names or any usage information anywhere.
- Settings, recent files, drafts and saved versions are stored locally in `~/Library/Application Support/Livemark/`.
- It contacts the network only when a document asks for it: to show an image that has a web address, to download such an image when **Apply above rules to online images** is on, or to open a link you click.
- Uploading images runs the uploader you configured, which sends those images to the service you chose. Livemark asks before the first upload.
- Spell checking and Quick Look use the services built into macOS.
